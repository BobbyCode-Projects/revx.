01 Problem statement

A patient call passes seven hand-offs. Each one can lose the appointment.

The ask began as a real-time call dashboard. The real need is wider: knowing whether each eligible patient inquiry ends in a booked visit, and where it breaks when it doesn't.

The business question: are we converting every eligible patient inquiry into a scheduled visit, and if not, where are we losing it?

02 Four business challenges

What we heard, translated into four management needs

Patient access visibility

Calls offered, answered, missed, abandoned and waiting, live.

Appointment conversion

Whether answered calls become booked visits, and why some don't.

Workforce performance

Agent productivity, workload and the reasons behind variation.

Revenue leakage

How many visits are lost, at which stage, and what it costs.

Answered ≠ booked. A handled call can still end without an appointment.
Missed ≠ lost revenue. Repeat callers and recovered inquiries must be separated out.

09 Integration & compliance

Voice APIs in. Insight out. No PHI, no EHR.

NovaChron RevX connects only to your telephony platform's Voice APIs. It reads call events, queue and agent states, and wrap-up codes. Patient records, clinical data and insurance details never leave your systems.

Cisco Finesse RESTWebex Contact Center APIsCTI eventsSIP / CDR feedsVoice REST APIsWebhooksWrap-up / disposition codesSSO / SAML

PHI-free by design

No EHR, clinical or insurance data. Caller numbers hashed on arrival. Recordings stay in your platform.

Data protection

US-hosted. AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit, role-based access, MFA, full audit trail.

Voice & outreach rules

Callback workflows respect TCPA consent and state call-recording consent laws.

HIPAA-aligned safeguards

Because a provider's call logs can still identify patients, we apply HIPAA Security Rule controls and sign a BAA if required.

API method depends on your telephony version and licensing, confirmed in discovery. Compliance controls are validated and documented during implementation.
